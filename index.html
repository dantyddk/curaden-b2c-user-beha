Metric definitions & calculation logic
New usersExactly 1 lifetime order; last purchase within 365 days of the data snapshot.
Repeat users2+ lifetime orders; last purchase within 365 days of the data snapshot.
Churn usersLast purchase more than 365 days before the data snapshot, regardless of order count.

Acquisition chart: All customers are counted in their first-order month, even if they are now Repeat or Churn. The New users KPI shows their current status.

Timing: Days to order 2, 3 and 4 are measured from the first order. Average and median gap describe each user's average gap, rather than all individual order intervals.

Order progression: Next-order rate = users who reached N+1 / users who reached N. Drop rate = 1 − next-order rate. These are observed cumulative rates, not forecasts; recent customers had less time to return.

Commercial metrics: Lifetime GMV and discount are in EUR. Discount is not total acquisition cost. Promo sensitivity uses the recorded source percentage.
